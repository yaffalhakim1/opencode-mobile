1. The turn arrives as it is written

Text, reasoning and tool calls share one panel, hairline parted, so a long run stays compact.

It streams while the app is backgrounded: you come back to the answer, not a spinner.

2. When it needs a decision, the turn stops

A question or permission request arrives as a card with its real choices.

Answer it and the turn carries on from where it paused.

3. The same question, answered at the desk or from the sofa

4. What the client will not do

  • No account A code your own server mints.
  • No traffic of its own Your LAN, or a tunnel you control.
  • No plaintext credential AES-256-GCM under a key in the Android keystore.
  • No analytics No crash reporting, no ad identifier, no push.
  • No model of its own The agent, the model and the effort come from your server.
  • No Play Store listing A signed APK per release, sideloaded.
